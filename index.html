Skills & tools

The toolkit, organised by what it does.

1. Data Analytics & BI

  • Dashboards & Reporting: Power BI and SQL dashboards tracking revenue, CAC and ROAS.
  • Data Cleaning & ETL: Python/Pandas processing of transaction, booking and ad-platform data.
  • Predictive Analytics: Python/Scikit-Learn classification models and NLP sentiment analysis on customer review data.
  • Customer Segmentation & RFM: behavior and purchase-pattern analysis to find high-value segments.

2. Forecasting & Commercial Strategy

  • Financial & Demand Forecasting: advanced Excel modelling for revenue, seasonality, staffing and procurement.
  • Pricing & Channel Optimization: pricing restructuring and distribution-channel recovery (Booking.com, Expedia).
  • Market & Competitor Research: executive briefings and gap analysis that end in a clear recommendation.
  • Stakeholder Mapping: MECE survey design and C-suite priority mapping for strategic recommendations.

3. Growth & Performance Marketing

  • Paid Media Architecture: Meta CBO consolidation, Advantage+ and learning-phase discipline to cut wasted spend.
  • Tracking & Attribution: Meta Pixel, Conversions API (CAPI) and Event Match Quality (EMQ) setup.
  • Full-Funnel Design: TOFU/MOFU/BOFU campaigns mapped to Eugene Schwartz's awareness stages.
  • Conversion Copywriting & CRO: AIDA, PAS and BAB frameworks, price anchoring and loss-framing, 3-second video hooks.

4. Automation & Systems

  • Workflow Automation: n8n and Python outreach engines and lead-enrichment tools.
  • Internal Tools: a Python/SQLite desktop app with audit trail for campaign outreach.
  • IT Foundation: BSc Information Technology, plus hands-on management of networks, databases and business systems.
  • Signal Measurement: high-intent signals (saves, shares, hold rate) over vanity metrics.
