How I turn insight into customer growth.

Acquisition & funnel performance

  • Meta ads: Campaign setup, audience selection, and budget management.
  • Ad tracking: Meta Pixel and Conversions API record actions after an ad interaction.
  • Campaign stages: Reach people discovering a hotel, considering a stay, or ready to book.
  • Ad writing: Develop messages for different audiences and booking-decision stages.
  • Booking experience: Present room choices and prices clearly; improve the path to direct booking.
  • Short-form video: Develop opening lines for mobile video ads.

Analytics & commercial decisions

  • Reports: Power BI and SQL to track customer costs, ad returns, and direct bookings.
  • Data preparation: Python and Pandas to clean booking, transaction, and advertising records.
  • Customer analysis: Review customer feedback and booking-interest patterns.
  • Forecasts: Excel models for revenue, demand, staffing, and purchasing.
  • Pricing and channels: Review hotel prices and sales through booking sites and direct websites.
  • Research: Use surveys and stakeholder mapping in business simulations.

IT & growth systems

  • Apps: Build Python desktop tools that support contact research and email work.
  • Connections: Connect apps to Outlook and public map data; gather website information while following crawler rules.
  • Data protection: Encrypt application data and package software for Windows.
  • IT foundation: Manage networks, Wi-Fi, databases, and property management systems.
