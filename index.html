Growth & analytics stack

Categorized capability matrix, not a flat tool list.

1. Paid Media & Campaign Architecture

  • Meta Ads Architecture: CBO consolidation, Advantage+ frameworks and learning-phase discipline to eliminate wasted spend.
  • Conversion Tracking & Attribution: Meta Pixel, Conversions API (CAPI) and Event Match Quality (EMQ) setup.
  • Full-Funnel Design: TOFU/MOFU/BOFU campaigns mapped to Eugene Schwartz's awareness stages.

2. Marketing Analytics & Data Engineering

  • Business Intelligence: Power BI & SQL dashboards tracking CAC, ROAS and direct revenue.
  • Data Cleaning & ETL: Python/Pandas processing of booking, transaction and ad-platform data.
  • Predictive Analytics: Python/Scikit-Learn classification models and NLP sentiment analysis on customer review data.
  • High-Intent Signal Measurement: Saves, shares and hold rate over vanity metrics.

3. Commercial Modeling & Strategy

  • Financial & Demand Forecasting: Advanced Excel modelling for revenue, seasonality and procurement.
  • Revenue Optimization & OTA Recovery: Pricing restructuring and OTA distribution recovery (Booking.com/Expedia).
  • Executive Stakeholder Mapping: MECE survey design and C-suite priority mapping for strategic recommendations.
  • Customer Segmentation & RFM: Guest behaviour and booking-pattern analysis for high-value personas.

4. Conversion Copywriting & Automation

  • Copywriting Frameworks: AIDA, PAS and BAB formulas for rapid multi-variant ad testing.
  • Behavioral Pricing: price anchoring, decoy pricing and loss-framing applied to hotel rate cards.
  • Hook Engineering: Scroll-stopping 3-second hooks written for 9:16 vertical video.
  • Workflow Automation: n8n and Python outreach engines and lead-enrichment tools.
