About me

IT Professional. Data & Business Intelligence.

A background in IT infrastructure that led into hospitality revenue systems and, now, a growth practice built on business intelligence, performance marketing and software I build myself.

01. The origin, technical grounding

I started out in IT installing systems, fixing networks, and troubleshooting databases. That gave me an understanding of how systems, not just software, fit together. I don't see marketing as art; I see it as a system with inputs, tracking and measurable outputs, a mindset that came from fixing infrastructure long before I touched an ad account.

02. The commercial shift, hospitality & revenue

Over close to two years at Sagana Getaway Resort, first as IT Assistant Manager, then IT Manager, I moved from fixing hardware to fixing revenue leaks. I took on the resort's social media, website and ad support, built Power BI and Excel forecasting models for demand and staffing, and found its Booking.com account had been deactivated over unpaid fees. I recovered it, restructured pricing around commission, and watched that one channel lift revenue by 13% within three months, growing to 20% of total revenue. That's when I knew growth and revenue work was where I wanted to build a career.

03. The scale, founder at StayFlow & Bloq Technologies

In 2026 I founded StayFlow to apply that same data-backed approach across multiple hospitality clients: audit tracking and data quality first, architect Meta CBO/CAPI campaigns second, model the financial outcome third. I lead a team of five freelancers and, after auditing more than 100 hotel properties, I'm currently working with 3 clients with 5 more in active talks.

Alongside the agency, I build standalone software under Bloq Technologies when nothing off-the-shelf solves a problem well enough; the projects include EmailIntel, a zero-budget contact-discovery system, and Success, a paced Outlook outreach platform. Both are used in my own and client prospecting.
