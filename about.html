About me

Growth work that starts with a question, not an ad.

A background in IT and hospitality operations that led into marketing, revenue and, now, customer acquisition and analytics.

I started out in IT installing systems, fixing networks, and helping people who were stuck. That job put me inside a real hotel's day-to-day operations, and I kept noticing the same thing: marketing decisions were being made without really understanding the guest, the competitor, or what was actually working.

Over close to two years at Sagana Getaway Resort first as IT Assistant Manager, then IT Manager I took on the resort's social media, website and ad support, and later found its Booking.com account had been deactivated over unpaid fees. I recovered it, renegotiated pricing, and watched that one channel lift revenue by 9% within three months. That's when I knew growth marketing was where I wanted to build a career.

In 2026 I founded StayFlow, a hospitality growth company built on one idea: research the guest and the competitor before spending a shilling on ads. I lead a five-person freelance team, audit hotels' digital presence, and build campaigns around real guest segments instead of generic advertising. After approaching more than 100 hotels, I'm currently working with 3 clients and have 5 more in active talks.

Outside client work, I co-founded Touch a Life Add a Smile, a community initiative supporting youth education the same underlying skills as client growth work: understanding an audience, building a plan around them, and finding the right partners to fund it. We've reached more than 1,400 students across three major events.
