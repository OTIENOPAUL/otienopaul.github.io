About me

An IT graduate who builds predictive models, dashboards and data pipelines.

My background spans IT operations, business intelligence and hospitality analytics. I use Python, SQL and Power BI to turn raw data into forecasts, risk scores and decisions, and I run StayFlow and Bloq Technologies alongside that work.

01. The origin, technical grounding

I started in IT, installing systems, fixing networks, and troubleshooting databases. That work taught me to look at how every part of a system affects the whole. I brought that same mindset into digital growth: understand the customer journey, measure the friction, and use data to improve the next decision.

02. The commercial shift, hospitality & revenue

For nearly two years at Sagana Getaway Resort, first as IT Assistant Manager and then IT Manager, I supported IT operations and commercial reporting. I built Power BI and Excel forecasting models for demand and staffing, and helped recover a deactivated Booking.com account. The related case study reports a 13% revenue increase over about three months and later contribution of about 20% of resort revenue from that channel; supporting records are not included in this portfolio repository.

03. The scale, founder at StayFlow & Bloq Technologies

In 2026 I founded StayFlow to help hospitality and service businesses improve lead generation, conversion performance, and customer retention. I review the quality of data and ad systems, assess funnel stages, and model commercial opportunities. The StayFlow case study describes an audit process, client work and a five-person freelance team; the client and property counts shown there are owner-reported and are not backed by records in this portfolio repository.

Alongside the agency, I build standalone software under Bloq Technologies when off-the-shelf tools cannot support the growth objective. The projects include EmailIntel, a zero-budget lead discovery system, and Success, a structured outreach platform that supports more efficient prospecting and pipeline progression.
