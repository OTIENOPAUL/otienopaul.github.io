Credit Risk Scoring & Default Prediction Engine

Problem
Lenders must decide quickly who may struggle to repay. Manual review is slow and inconsistent, and defaults are rare, so a naive model can look accurate while missing the risky minority.
Approach
Trained a regularized LightGBM classifier with class-imbalance handling, audited it with SHAP, and served it through a Streamlit app that scores applicant profiles.
Outcome
A prototype interface illustrates applicant inputs, a 12% threshold and a sample 11.64% score. The sample is illustrative, not a validated lending decision.
  • Python
  • LightGBM
  • scikit-learn
  • SHAP
  • Streamlit

CFO Dashboard: Revenue, Profitability & Growth

Problem
Revenue alone hides segments that sell a lot but earn little. Finance leaders need sales and profit side by side.
Approach
Cleaned currency-formatted fields and built KPI cards, trend, country and segment visuals in Power BI to compare scale with profit.
Outcome
Exposed that Enterprise sells 19.6M but earns only ~0.16M profit, while Government leads on both. A clear case for revisiting pricing and discounts.

EmailIntel: B2B Contact Discovery & Validation Engine

Problem
Finding the right contact at each target company was manual and slow, with no honest way to tell a confirmed email from a guess. Paid enrichment tools were not viable on a zero budget.
Approach
Built a pipeline that discovers companies, crawls public pages, learns each company’s email pattern, validates MX records and scores every result with its source.
Outcome
The technical notes describe a source-backed contact-discovery workflow and test cases; test execution was not independently confirmed, and time saved was not measured.
  • Python
  • SQLite
  • Web scraping
  • MX validation

Multi-Location Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

Problem
A three-store coffee business needed to know what sells, where and when.
Approach
Analyzed 149,116 transactions in Power BI, estimating sales as quantity × price and comparing product, store and hour.
Outcome
Coffee and tea make up about two thirds of sales, stores are nearly level, and trade peaks sharply in the morning (10:00 strongest).
