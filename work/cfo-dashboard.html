Power BI · Executive finance overview

CFO Dashboard

A Power BI finance dashboard for comparing sales, profit, margin, and units across time, customer groups, countries, and products.

View project on GitHubAll case studies
In this projectData usedWhat the data showsWhy it is usefulData notes

Data used

700 rows from 2013–2014 in Financials.csv, with no missing values in the supplied file. Currency-formatted fields were cleaned for calculation.

What the data shows

  • Net sales total approximately $118.73 million. Gross sales are approximately $127.93 million, with $9.21 million in discounts.
  • Recorded profit is approximately $17.67 million, about 14.9% of net sales.
  • Government is the largest segment by sales at $52.50 million and profit at $11.39 million.
  • Enterprise records $19.61 million in sales but approximately $162,776 in profit, a much thinner margin than the other segments.

Why it is useful

The report puts sales and profit beside one another, making it easier to compare segment scale with the profit each segment contributes.

Data notes

Figures are calculated from the adjacent CSV and rounded. The source uses currency values but does not explicitly identify the currency. The report layout supports the described views.
