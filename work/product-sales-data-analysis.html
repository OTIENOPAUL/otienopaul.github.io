Product Sales Data Analysis

A Power BI analysis of product sales and profit across products, countries, customer groups, discount bands, and time.

View project on GitHubAll case studies
In this projectData usedWhat the data showsWhy it is usefulData notes

Data used

The supplied Financial Sample.xlsx contains 700 rows across 2013–2014, with six products, five countries, and five customer groups.

What the data shows

  • The workbook records approximately $118.73 million in sales, 1.13 million units sold, and $16.89 million in profit.
  • Government is the largest customer group at $52.50 million in sales and $11.39 million in profit.
  • Paseo leads the products by sales at $33.01 million and profit at $4.80 million.
  • The United States has the highest country sales at $25.03 million, narrowly ahead of Canada at $24.89 million.
  • 2014 has $92.31 million in sales versus $26.42 million in 2013, but also has three times as many records. The year comparison reflects uneven data coverage.

Why it is useful

The analysis compares product, market, and customer-group performance while keeping the difference in record coverage visible when reading year totals.

Data notes

Profit in this workbook is about $16.89 million; the separate Finance CSV has about $17.67 million despite similar sales totals. These are separate source files and should not be combined without investigating the discrepancy. Currency is not identified explicitly.
