StayFlow Outreach Engine

A desktop tool I built to automate and track personalised outreach emails to hotel prospects, replacing a manual spreadsheet process.

Outreach engine

The problem

Finding hotel prospects was manual: a spreadsheet, a lot of copy-pasting, and no reliable way to track who had been contacted, who replied, and who to follow up with.

What I built

A desktop application (Python, SQLite) that imports a prospect list, merges fields into a reusable email template, and tracks every prospect through a pending → contacted → replied state stored locally, so nothing is lost if the app closes mid-run.

The result

Replaced a fully manual spreadsheet workflow with a repeatable, trackable outreach process, now used as the first touch-point in StayFlow's own client pipeline alongside the direct-booking growth engine →.
