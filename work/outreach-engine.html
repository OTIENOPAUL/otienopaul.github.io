StayFlow Outreach Engine

A password-protected desktop app I built to automate how StayFlow reaches out to prospective hotel clients.

Outreach engine

The problem

Reaching 80+ hotels meant manually typing an email template and subject line into every row of a spreadsheet, then running a script to build drafts. The spreadsheet never updated after emails actually sent, so there was no reliable record of who'd already been contacted, and no audit trail if something went wrong.

What I built

I rebuilt the workflow as a desktop app. You pick a lead list, an email template and a subject line once per campaign — the app builds personalised drafts in Outlook for every selected lead, lets you preview a sample or send a test email first, and shows exactly who's eligible before anything is created. Nothing sends automatically: drafts land in Outlook for review and only go out once I click "Send Reviewed Drafts."

Underneath, the spreadsheet stays a clean lead database, each lead's contact status updates automatically as emails send, and full campaign history is logged in a local database, with a hashed application password and encrypted local storage for sensitive settings.

The result

A manual, error-prone process became a single, repeatable campaign screen — with the spreadsheet and the actual send history never falling out of sync. It's the tool behind StayFlow's outreach to prospective hotel clients, the same growth process described in the research-driven growth case study.
