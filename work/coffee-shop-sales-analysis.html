Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

A Power BI analysis of coffee shop transactions, focused on product mix, store performance, and the hours when customers buy.

View project on GitHubAll case studies
In this projectData usedWhat the data showsWhy it is usefulData notes

Data used

149,116 transactions from January through June 2023 across three New York locations, recorded in Coffee Shop Sales.xlsx. The records include 214,470 items sold.

What the data shows

  • Estimated gross sales are approximately $698,812 for the period, calculated from item quantity multiplied by unit price.
  • Coffee contributes about $269,952 and tea about $196,406; together they make up roughly two thirds of recorded sales.
  • Hell’s Kitchen has the highest location total at about $236,511, followed by Astoria at $232,244 and Lower Manhattan at $230,057. The location totals are close.
  • The busiest hours by gross sales are 10:00, 09:00, and 08:00, showing a strong morning peak in this six-month sample.

Why it is useful

A manager can compare sales by product, location, and hour to understand where demand is concentrated and when the busiest trading period occurs.

Data notes

These are gross sales estimates, not profit. The report package did not expose a readable standard layout during inspection, so specific report pages and measure definitions could not be independently confirmed.
