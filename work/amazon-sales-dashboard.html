Amazon Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI report for tracking revenue, profit, orders, ratings, and sales patterns across product groups, regions, payment methods, and discount levels.

View project on GitHubAll case studies
In this projectData usedWhat the data showsWhy it is usefulData notes

Data used

50,000 order records and 14 fields from 2022–2023, supplied in cleaned_dataset.csv. The supplied file has no missing values.

What the data shows

  • Recorded revenue totals 32.87 million and recorded profit totals 1.64 million across the two years. The source does not identify a currency.
  • Revenue was nearly flat year over year: 16.39 million in 2022 and 16.48 million in 2023, about 0.5% growth.
  • Beauty records the largest category revenue at 5.55 million; Sports records the smallest at 5.41 million. Category totals are closely grouped.
  • The Middle East leads the four regions at 8.30 million in revenue. Average order-line revenue declines across the listed discount levels; this is a descriptive pattern, not proof discounts caused the change.
  • Average product rating is approximately 3.0 out of 5.

Why it is useful

The report brings overall performance and its breakdowns into one view, helping a reader compare time periods, product groups, regions, payment methods, and discount levels.

Data notes

Figures are calculated from the adjacent CSV. The report layout supports the visual topics described. Currency is not specified in the source.
