Strategic Growth & Financing Model Evaluation

BeyondTech Strategy Consulting simulation (Forage) evaluating customer preferences and executive priorities for a new mobile device installment financing model.

Financing model evaluation

The context

BeyondTech, a technology retailer, wanted to know whether an installment financing model for mobile devices was viable, and if so, how it should be structured and positioned across global markets.

What I did

Survey engineering: structured a 14-question MECE consumer survey using trade-off analysis to test interest rates, contract lengths and down-payment priorities.

Data & trend analysis: analyzed responses from 450 global participants, identifying the core target demographic (ages 25–44, $25k–$99k income) and a ~79% purchase-intent uplift when financing was offered.

Stakeholder alignment: profiled the CFO, CMO, CTO, Sales and COO to map each one's motivators and concerns, then reconciled capital risk against brand reputation and technical scalability.

The finding

Customer demand dropped sharply above a 10% APR, and 0%-interest terms mattered far more to adoption than a lower down payment. I used that to frame a transparent 0% financing recommendation that satisfied the CFO's risk limits, the CMO's brand-trust goals, and Sales' push for a low-friction close.
