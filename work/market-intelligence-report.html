Market Intelligence: East African Hospitality Report

"Navigating Shifted Demand", a self-directed executive briefing on shifting guest demand and where legacy hotels are losing ground to modern, agile properties.

Market intelligence deck

The deliverable

A self-directed executive presentation, "Navigating Shifted Demand: A Comparative Analysis of Domestic Hospitality Marketing Strategies in Kenya," built to turn market observation and research into a hireable, presentable proof of strategic thinking.

Core focus

Market overview: tracking the shift from traditional international-tourist dependency toward domestic staycationers and lifestyle travel.

Strategic gap analysis: why legacy, city-based properties are losing bookings to agile, boutique and experience-led stays.

Customer acquisition breakdown: visual comparison of low-converting traditional ads against high-converting direct-booking social funnels.
