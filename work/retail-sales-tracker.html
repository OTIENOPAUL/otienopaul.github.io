Retail Sales Tracker

An interactive Power BI dashboard for monitoring sales, transactions, customer counts, quantities, and customer mix over time.

View project on GitHubAll case studies
In this projectData usedWhat the data showsWhy it is usefulData notes

Data used

1,000 transactions from January through December 2023 in retail_sales_dataset.csv. The file includes three product categories and has no missing values.

What the data shows

  • Recorded sales total 456,000 across 1,000 transactions, averaging 456 per transaction. The source does not identify a currency.
  • Electronics leads category sales at 156,905, closely followed by clothing at 155,580; beauty records 143,515.
  • The supplied data has 510 female and 490 male transactions. Their sales totals and average transaction amounts are close.
  • Customer ages range from 18 to 64, with a mean age of about 41.4.

Why it is useful

The dashboard makes it possible to compare monthly sales and product categories alongside transaction volume and the customer fields present in the dataset.

Data notes

The recorded Total Amount field is used as supplied. Currency is not specified, and the analysis makes no customer-level claims beyond the available fields. The report layout supports the described topics.
