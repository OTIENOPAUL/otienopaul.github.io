Direct Booking Growth & Behavioral CRO Framework

A four-stage framework for an independent city hotel. It uses clear room choices, pricing, and a simpler booking process to encourage guests to book directly instead of through third-party booking sites.

Behavioral CRO framework

The planning scenario

This scenario describes an independent 20–30 room city hotel. It assumes the hotel pays 15–25% commission on bookings made through third-party booking sites, while its own website converts poorly.

This is a proposed framework, not a live test. The case study does not report measured changes in bookings or revenue.

The four-stage framework

1. Build trust: show real guest-experience photos, ratings, certifications, and press coverage near the start of the page.

2. Make room prices easier to compare: show three room options, explain what comes with a direct booking, and clearly state any third-party booking fee.

3. Make booking easier on a phone: offer a short mobile checkout, a wallet payment option, and a 24-hour rate hold.

4. Stay in touch before arrival: use the confirmation page and a three-part email sequence to remind guests of their booking and the savings.
