The problem

How can hotel and restaurant management understand revenue performance, customer purchasing patterns and the operational drivers of sales?

Without a single view, decisions about staffing, stock and promotions lack a clear picture of when demand peaks, what sells and how guests pay.

How I solved it

  1. Cleaned and structured the bar and restaurant transaction data in Excel and loaded it into Power BI.
  2. Built DAX measures for total revenue, net revenue, average bill value and quantity sold.
  3. Designed a one-page report with year and source (bar or restaurant) slicers and visuals for day of week, monthly revenue, menu items, modes of payment, waiter sales and quantity sold.
  4. Turned patterns into recommendations a manager can act on, such as where to add staff and stock.

The outcome

  • Headline KPIs at a glance: 15.06M total revenue, 13.72M net revenue, a 3.37K average bill and 22K items sold.
  • Saturday generated the strongest daily revenue, pointing to a concentration of weekend demand.
  • Recommendation: increase weekend staffing, inventory allocation and promotional activity around peak periods.
  • Staff and product visibility: waiter-level sales and menu-item rankings in the same report.
