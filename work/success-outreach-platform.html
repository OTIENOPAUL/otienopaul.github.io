Success, Personalized Outreach Platform

A Windows desktop app for personalized outreach email at scale, built on the user's own Outlook account, without it turning into spam, and without any paid or subscription infrastructure.

Success, Bloq Technologies

The problem

Outreach at any real volume from a personal Outlook account risks two failure modes: get flagged as spam from sending too fast, or lose track of who was contacted, who bounced, and who already opted out, especially across multiple campaigns run over weeks.

What I built

A full desktop application with prospect import, template creation, message drafting, paced sending and bounce cleanup, built around one rule: nothing sends without a person clicking Send, and every send is re-checked against live conditions, not just validated once at the start.

Key outcomes

  • Enabled spam-safe outreach at scale with zero paid infrastructure by building drafting and sending directly on the user's own Outlook desktop account, with randomized 60–90s pacing between sends instead of a bulk-email service.
  • Eliminated accidental over-sending through three independent checks re-verified before every single send by gating each send on a configurable daily cap, a live connectivity test (not just the OS's "connected" flag), and a working-hours window, so a resumed campaign never fires a burst of email at 2 a.m.
  • Removed the risk of lost or duplicated sends on a crash by persisting every prospect's state (pending → drafted → sent / bounced / skipped / failed) in a local SQLite database, so an interrupted campaign resumes exactly where it left off, never re-sending.
  • Cut pre-send spam-filter risk by building a live template preview per prospect that flags wording known to trip spam filters. The warning is advisory, never blocking, so the sender stays in control.
  • Reduced manual list hygiene to a short review step by building an inbox bounce-scanner that reads delivery-failure notices and proposes affected addresses for removal, with nothing auto-deleted.
  • Prevented duplicate or unwanted contact across campaigns by routing every prospect through an opt-out and cooldown check before a draft is ever created.

Why it's resumable

State lives in SQLite, not in memory, with foreign keys, a busy timeout, WAL journaling and secure delete enabled, so campaign data survives an app crash or a closed laptop lid without corruption.
