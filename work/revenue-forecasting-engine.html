The problem

Sagana Getaway Resort suffered from unpredictable seasonal demand, which led to inefficient staffing and over- or under-procurement heading into peak and off-peak periods, with no reliable way to plan ahead.

How I solved it

  1. Analyzed over a year of historical transaction data in Excel.
  2. Built a multi-variable scenario model covering best-case and worst-case demand, using XLOOKUP and dynamic arrays.
  3. Fed the outputs into Power BI dashboards used to inform staffing and procurement decisions.

Skills used

  • Advanced Excel
  • XLOOKUP & dynamic arrays
  • Power BI
  • Time-series analysis
  • Scenario modeling

The outcome

  • Reported about 95% forecasting accuracy for demand, labor allocation and procurement over a five-month period.
  • A repeatable planning process the resort could act on ahead of time instead of reacting to seasons.
