Demand & Operational Forecasting Engine

A multi-variable Excel and Power BI model that reached ~95% forecasting accuracy for demand, labor allocation and procurement at Sagana Getaway Resort.

Forecasting engine

The challenge

Sagana Getaway Resort suffered from unpredictable seasonal demand, leading to inefficient staffing and over- or under-procurement heading into peak and off-peak periods, with no reliable way to plan ahead.

The data & process

I analyzed over a year of historical transaction data in Excel and built a multi-variable scenario model covering best-case and worst-case demand, feeding the outputs into Power BI dashboards used to inform staffing and procurement decisions in real time.

The impact

The model achieved ~95% forecasting accuracy for demand, labor allocation and procurement over a five-month period turning seasonal guesswork into a repeatable planning process the resort could act on ahead of time.

Where else this applies

The method (historical time-series data, best- and worst-case scenarios, live dashboards for staffing and purchasing decisions) applies to any business with seasonal or volatile demand: retail and e-commerce inventory, logistics capacity, contact-centre staffing or subscription revenue planning.
