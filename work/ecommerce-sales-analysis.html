Power BI · Orders, profit, and payment patterns

E-commerce Sales Analysis

A Power BI analysis joining order-line sales and profit with customer location and order dates to examine product mix, payments, and loss-making lines.

Data used

The supplied 2018 files contain 1,500 order lines in Details.csv and 500 orders in Orders.csv. All detail rows matched an order in the supplied files.

What the data shows

  • Recorded sales total 437,771 and profit totals 36,963, for an overall margin of about 8.4%. The source does not specify a currency.
  • 529 of the 1,500 order lines show a loss, so positive total profit hides a notable share of loss-making lines.
  • Electronics records the most sales at 166,267. Clothing records the highest category profit at 13,325.
  • Cash on delivery is the largest payment mode by sales at 155,181. Maharashtra has the highest state-level sales in the supplied data at 102,498.

Why it is useful

The combined view lets a reader compare sales and profit by product, payment method, and location, while identifying order lines that may need a closer look.

Data notes

Figures are calculated from the adjacent CSV files. Amounts use the source’s unspecified currency. Report pages and measure logic could not be independently confirmed from the Power BI package.
