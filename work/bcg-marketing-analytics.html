Multi-Channel Marketing Campaign & Conversion Optimization

BCG Data for Decision Makers simulation (Forage) evaluating a consumer goods client's digital ad spend across channels to improve conversion efficiency.

Multi-channel marketing analytics

The brief

A consumer goods client needed its paid digital advertising across Social, Search, Display and Video evaluated, to reallocate spend and improve conversion efficiency.

What I did

Funnel & cost diagnostics: tracked impression-to-click and click-to-conversion rates per channel, and calculated CPC, CPA and ROAS.

Customer segmentation: cross-tabulated demographic segments against ad creative and placement to separate high-LTV converting audiences from underperforming spend.

Budget re-allocation: proposed a revised spend distribution to raise overall conversion yield while lowering CPA.

The result

The analysis isolated specific high-cost channels underperforming against segments with much higher conversion efficiency, turning into a concrete reallocation proposal.
