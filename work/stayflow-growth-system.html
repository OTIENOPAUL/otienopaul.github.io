Research-Driven Hotel Growth

The audit-first approach behind every StayFlow campaign — built on competitor research and guest segmentation, not generic hotel ads.

StayFlow method

The problem

Most hotels I approached were running the same ad to every visitor — one generic promotion, with no distinction between a family booking a weekend getaway and a company booking a conference. Marketing spend was going out with no clear link to who was actually likely to book.

My approach

A repeatable process I now run for every StayFlow client: first, an audit of the hotel's current marketing, website and OTA presence. Then research into competitor hotels in the same market — pricing, positioning, offers and content. From there, I define real guest segments — families, corporate travel, seasonal visitors, returning guests — and build campaigns and messaging for each one, running paid media through Meta and Google and tracking performance against bookings, not impressions.

The result

This approach is what StayFlow is built around. After approaching more than 100 hotels with it, I'm currently working with 3 clients and have 5 more in active discussions, leading a five-person freelance team to deliver the process for each one.
