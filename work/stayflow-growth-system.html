Performance Marketing & Conversion Architecture

The StayFlow Direct-Booking Growth Engine

Growth audit and digital engine shifting hotel guest acquisition from OTAs to direct channels, built on consolidated Meta CBO architecture and CAPI tracking, not generic hotel ads.

StayFlow growth engine

The challenge

Mid-tier hotels in Kenya lose up to 20% of revenue to OTA commissions while running unstructured, generic social media ads, one promotion to every visitor, with no tracking infrastructure reliable enough to prove what was actually working.

The strategy & execution

Tracking: implemented Meta Conversions API (CAPI) alongside Pixel tracking to restore server-side event data and push Event Match Quality (EMQ) above 8.0.

Campaign setup: replaced fragmented ad sets with consolidated CBO campaigns using Advantage+ placement, run with learning-phase discipline.

Creative strategy: applied the 5 Stages of Awareness to target local staycationers, paired with PAS-driven ad copy and 3-second hook scripting for 9:16 vertical Reels.

Automation: integrated n8n and WhatsApp booking flows, connected to the same outreach pipeline behind the StayFlow Outreach Engine →.

The result

This is the repeatable engine every StayFlow client runs on. After auditing more than 100 hotel properties with it, I'm currently working with 3 clients and have 5 more in active discussions, leading a five-person freelance team to deliver the process for each one.
