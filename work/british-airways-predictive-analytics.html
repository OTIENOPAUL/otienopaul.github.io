Predictive Consumer Analytics & Sentiment Modeling

British Airways Data Science simulation (Forage) uncovering drivers of customer sentiment and predicting booking behavior from historical journey data.

Predictive analytics & NLP

The challenge

British Airways receives thousands of customer reviews and booking interactions daily. The task was to find the core drivers of customer sentiment and build a model to predict booking/buying behavior from that data.

What I did

Review scraping & NLP: scraped customer review data with Python, cleaned and tokenized the text, removed stop words, applied lemmatization, and scored sentiment (VADER/TextBlob) alongside word-cloud and topic modeling to surface recurring complaints like flight delays and cabin service.

Predictive modeling: engineered features from booking lead time, trip duration, extra baggage requests and preferred departure day, then trained and compared classification models (Random Forest, XGBoost, Logistic Regression) to predict booking completion.

Evaluation: assessed models on ROC-AUC, precision, recall and feature importance to keep the results interpretable, not just accurate.

The result

Feature importance analysis identified booking lead time and extra baggage selection as leading conversion signals, which I translated into an executive-ready summary of strategic growth levers rather than a raw model readout.
