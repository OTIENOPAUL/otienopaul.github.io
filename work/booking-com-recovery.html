Booking.com Revenue Recovery

Recovering a deactivated OTA account and redesigning pricing at Sagana Getaway Resort leading to a 9% revenue lift within three months.

OTA recovery

The problem

While reviewing the resort's revenue and marketing channels, I found that its Booking.com listing had been deactivated over unpaid commission fees. A channel that should have been bringing in bookings had gone completely dark, and no one had flagged it.

My approach

I contacted Booking.com directly to recover account access, then negotiated a payment arrangement so the property could resume taking bookings before the outstanding balance was fully cleared. Once the account was live again, I reviewed the resort's OTA pricing and redesigned it, including a promotional structure, so bookings through the channel stayed profitable after commission rather than repeating the pricing mistakes that had contributed to the account falling behind in the first place.

The result

Within about three months of reactivation, Booking.com contributed to a 9% increase in total revenue. As the channel matured, it went on to represent roughly a fifth of the resort's total revenue turning a dead booking channel into one of its most reliable sources of guests.
