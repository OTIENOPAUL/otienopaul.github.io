Sales Channel Recovery & Margin Restructuring

Reactivating a deactivated OTA channel and restructuring room pricing to absorb commission costs at Sagana Getaway Resort — a 13% revenue lift within three months.

OTA revenue recovery

The challenge

Sagana Getaway Resort's Booking.com account had been deactivated over unpaid back commissions, shutting off a key international booking channel entirely — a channel that should have been bringing in bookings had gone completely dark, and no one had flagged it.

The execution

I negotiated a structured payout plan directly with Booking.com to restore account access before the outstanding balance was fully settled. Once the account was live again, I restructured the resort's room pricing formulas to absorb OTA commission costs without eroding net margins, rather than repeating the pricing mistakes that had contributed to the account falling behind in the first place.

The impact

The channel was restored within days. Within about three months of reactivation, Booking.com drove a 13% revenue lift, and as the channel matured it went on to represent roughly 20% of the resort's total revenue turning a dead booking channel into one of its most reliable sources of guests.

Where else this applies

Recovering a lapsed distribution channel and pricing around partner commission is the same problem any business faces when selling through marketplaces or resellers, from retail marketplaces to travel and delivery platforms.
