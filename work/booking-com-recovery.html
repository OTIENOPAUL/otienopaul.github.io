The problem

Sagana Getaway Resort’s Booking.com account had been deactivated over unpaid back commissions. A key international booking channel was switched off entirely, and no one had flagged it.

How I solved it

  1. Diagnosed the cause: unpaid back commissions had triggered the deactivation.
  2. Negotiated a structured payout plan directly with Booking.com to restore access before the outstanding balance was fully settled.
  3. Rebuilt the room pricing formulas so the resort absorbs OTA commission costs without eroding net margins.
  4. Tracked the channel’s revenue as it matured.

Skills used

  • OTA distribution strategy
  • Pricing & revenue math
  • Vendor negotiation
  • Margin analysis

The outcome

  • Channel restored within days.
  • Reported 13% revenue lift within about three months of reactivation.
  • Reported about 20% of total resort revenue came from the channel as it matured.
